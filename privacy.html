Privacy Policy

Effective 15 August 2026 · ferasedit@gmail.com

01

Information we collect

When you authorize a reputequran integration to connect to your TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube account, we receive only what that platform's login flow shares by default:

  • Basic public profile information: display name, username, and profile photo
  • An access token scoped to the specific permission you approved (for example, publishing a video on your behalf)

We do not collect passwords: authentication happens entirely on the platform's own login screen, and we never see or store your credentials.

02

How we use it

Information is used solely to operate the connection you requested: authenticating your account and publishing content you have explicitly approved to that account. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what you authorized.

In short: nothing is sold, nothing is shared for marketing, and every connected action traces back to something you approved.
03

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with third parties except the platform the data originated from, as part of normal API operation, or where required by law.

04

Retention and deletion

We retain connection information only for as long as the integration remains active. You can revoke access at any time from the relevant platform's app-permissions settings, or by emailing us to request deletion; we will confirm removal within a reasonable time.

05

Security

Access tokens are stored using industry-standard encrypted infrastructure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the sensitivity of the information involved.

06

Children's privacy

Our content and any account-connection integration are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

07

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or a request to delete your data, can be sent to ferasedit@gmail.com. See our Terms of Service for the rules governing our content.
